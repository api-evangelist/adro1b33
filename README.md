ADRO, Inc. (에이드로) is a South Korean mobility aerotech company in Yongin-si, Gyeonggi-do that designs and manufactures aerospace-grade carbon-fiber aerodynamic components for global automotive brands, and that has extended into software with AOX (Aerodynamic Optimization eXperience) — a web-based, GPU- and HPC-backed aerodynamic design and optimization platform operated at aoxlabs.com that takes a CAD or STL geometry through meshing, CFD simulation, shape optimization and post-processing without requiring CFD expertise. The AOX platform is backed by a public Django REST Framework contract at api.aoxlabs.com serving a 231-operation OpenAPI 3.0.3 document covering projects, jobs, LES runs, sketch sessions, patches, artifacts, assets, teams, credits, plans, subscriptions and payments. The company's US direct-to-consumer parts storefront at adro.com is a Shopify store that serves an agent-facing llms.txt and a live Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) MCP endpoint.

**Areas:** Aerodynamics · Computational Fluid Dynamics · Simulation · Automotive · Engineering · Optimization · Manufacturing · Artificial Intelligence · High Performance Computing · E-Commerce · MCP · South Korea

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for ADRO: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
